Athletes Programme

The Jungle BJJ athletes programme provides free acommodation to athletes that want to train full-time.
The programme runs from June to November, which is the dry season in Kuranda, North Queensland.
We provide a clean, safe environment for individuals to completely imerse themselves in full time training.
Located on jungle jams ranch, a large pristine property set amongst the oldest rain forest on planet earth with
an array of rare and endangered wildlife and botanical brilliance. The rooms are large and oppulent.
We do NOT provide food or transport, but all other bills are covered including transportation and competition
fees to local BJJ tornments and super fights. All lineages are accepted and athletes are free to compete under
their prospective academies name. We encourage members to utilize the spectacular surroundings to promote their
prospective social media accounts and build their careers. During your stay on jungle jams ranch there will be various
social events, activities and enterteinment. If you are looking to be a part of this programme please contact Jungle BJJ Team
to book your limited position on the proramme.
